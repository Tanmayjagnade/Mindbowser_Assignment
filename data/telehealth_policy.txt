Telehealth Consultation Guidelines

Telehealth is available for follow-up consultations and non-emergency concerns.
Patients may request medication refills during telehealth visits if the medication is already prescribed.
Controlled substances generally require additional in-person evaluation per clinician discretion.
New severe symptoms such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, or stroke warning signs require emergency care and should not be handled through telehealth.
Telehealth sessions must be conducted over approved secure video platforms.
