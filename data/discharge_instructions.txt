PATIENT DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS
MedAssist Healthcare System — General Care Guidelines
Document Version: 2024-01

OVERVIEW
These discharge instructions are provided to help patients recover safely after hospitalization. Please read them carefully and follow all instructions before leaving the facility. If you have any questions, speak with your nurse or attending physician before discharge.

MEDICATIONS
- Take all prescribed medications exactly as directed.
- Do not skip doses, even if you feel better.
- If you experience side effects such as rash, difficulty breathing, or severe dizziness, stop the medication and call your doctor immediately.
- Do not take over-the-counter medications, supplements, or herbal remedies without consulting your physician first.
- Keep all medications in their original containers and store them at the temperature indicated on the label.

WOUND CARE
- Keep surgical incisions or wounds clean and dry for at least 48 hours after discharge.
- Change dressings as instructed by your nurse. Wash hands thoroughly before and after touching the wound area.
- Watch for signs of infection: increasing redness, warmth, swelling, pus or unusual discharge, or fever above 101°F (38.3°C).
- Do not submerge wounds in water (bathing, swimming) until cleared by your doctor.

ACTIVITY RESTRICTIONS
- Avoid heavy lifting (over 10 lbs) for the period specified by your physician.
- Do not drive a vehicle while taking opioid pain medications or sedatives.
- Gradually increase activity levels as tolerated. Rest when you feel fatigued.
- Patients who underwent abdominal or chest surgery should avoid strenuous exercise for at least 4-6 weeks.

DIET AND NUTRITION
- Follow any special diet prescribed by your care team (low sodium, low fat, diabetic, etc.).
- Stay well hydrated by drinking 6-8 glasses of water per day unless instructed otherwise.
- Avoid alcohol for at least 48 hours after anesthesia and while taking prescription medications.
- If you experience nausea after discharge, eat small, bland meals (crackers, toast, rice) and gradually return to a normal diet.

FOLLOW-UP APPOINTMENTS
- Schedule your follow-up appointment within 7-14 days of discharge, or as specifically instructed by your physician.
- Bring all current medications to your follow-up visit.
- If you have been given a referral to a specialist, contact their office within 5 business days.
- Do not miss follow-up appointments. Many complications are caught early only through scheduled monitoring.

WHEN TO SEEK EMERGENCY CARE
Go to the nearest emergency room or call 911 immediately if you experience:
- Chest pain or pressure
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Sudden numbness or weakness on one side of the body
- Sudden severe headache
- High fever (above 103°F / 39.4°C)
- Uncontrolled bleeding
- Confusion, loss of consciousness, or seizures
- Signs of severe allergic reaction (swollen throat, hives, difficulty swallowing)

CONTACT INFORMATION
For non-emergency questions, call the MedAssist Patient Support Line: 1800-MED-ASSIST (available 24/7).
For prescription renewals, contact your primary care physician's office.
