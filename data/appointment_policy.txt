APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING POLICY
MedAssist Healthcare System
Document Version: 2024-01

PURPOSE
This policy establishes guidelines for scheduling, confirming, cancelling, and rescheduling patient appointments at MedAssist Healthcare System facilities.

SCHEDULING AN APPOINTMENT
Patients may schedule appointments through the following channels:
- Online: Patient portal at medassist.example.com/appointments (available 24/7)
- Phone: Call 1800-MED-ASSIST, Monday to Friday 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- In-person: Visit the registration desk at any MedAssist facility
- Telehealth: Available for eligible visits via the online portal or mobile app

New patients must register before scheduling. Registration requires a valid government-issued photo ID, insurance card (if applicable), and a completed New Patient Registration Form.

APPOINTMENT TYPES
- Routine/Preventive: Annual physicals, wellness visits, vaccinations (2-4 weeks advance booking recommended)
- Follow-up: Post-hospitalization or specialist follow-up (typically within 7-14 days)
- Urgent: Same-day or next-day slots for patients with acute non-emergency symptoms
- Emergency: Walk-ins accepted; triaged immediately
- Telehealth: Follow-up visits, minor illness consultations, medication reviews, and mental health check-ins

APPOINTMENT CONFIRMATION
All appointments confirmed by:
- Automated SMS reminder 48 hours before appointment
- Email confirmation sent immediately after scheduling
- Phone call for high-priority or surgical appointments

Patients should confirm or cancel at least 24 hours in advance.

CANCELLATION POLICY
- Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a cancellation fee.
- A no-show fee of $25 may be applied for missed appointments without prior notice.
- Three or more no-shows within 12 months may result in booking restrictions.
- Emergency cancellations (illness, hospitalization) are exempt when documentation is provided.

RESCHEDULING
- Patients may reschedule up to 3 times per slot before a new booking is required.
- Reschedule requests within 24 hours will be accommodated based on availability.

WAIT TIMES
- Routine appointments: Average 5-10 business days.
- Specialist appointments: 1-3 weeks depending on specialty.
- Urgent care slots: Available same day or next day.
- Emergency walk-ins: Triaged within 15 minutes of arrival.

LATE ARRIVAL POLICY
Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to reschedule.

SPECIAL ACCOMMODATIONS
Patients requiring wheelchair access, interpreter services, or other accommodations should notify the scheduling team at least 48 hours in advance. Interpreter services available for over 20 languages at no charge.

CONTACT
Appointment inquiries: 1800-MED-ASSIST or appointments@medassist.example.com
