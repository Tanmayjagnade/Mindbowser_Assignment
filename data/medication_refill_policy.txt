MEDICATION REFILL POLICY
MedAssist Healthcare System
Document Version: 2024-01

PURPOSE
This policy outlines the process for requesting prescription medication refills at MedAssist Healthcare System to ensure patient safety and continuity of care.

ELIGIBLE MEDICATIONS FOR REFILL
Refills are available for:
- Chronic disease medications (hypertension, diabetes, thyroid, asthma, etc.) prescribed and managed at MedAssist
- Mental health medications under active management by a MedAssist provider
- Other medications with active prescriptions issued by a MedAssist physician

INELIGIBLE FOR REFILL WITHOUT IN-PERSON EVALUATION
The following categories require an in-person evaluation or telehealth visit before refills are approved:
- Controlled substances (opioids, benzodiazepines, stimulants) — subject to DEA and state regulations
- Antibiotics and antifungals (require evaluation to confirm ongoing need)
- Medications with significant side effects requiring monitoring (e.g., blood thinners, chemotherapy agents)
- Any medication where the original prescription has expired

HOW TO REQUEST A REFILL
1. Patient Portal: Log in to medassist.example.com/portal and select "Request Refill" under your active prescriptions.
2. Phone: Call 1800-MED-REFILL during business hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
3. Pharmacy Direct: Many pharmacies can request refills directly from your provider on your behalf with a signed authorization.
4. Telehealth: Medication refill reviews can be conducted during a telehealth visit if the medication is non-controlled and does not require physical examination.

PROCESSING TIME
- Routine refill requests: Processed within 2-3 business days.
- Urgent refills: Same-day processing is available for critical medications (e.g., insulin, heart medications) when the request is submitted before 12:00 PM.
- Controlled substances: Require an appointment (in-person or telehealth) and cannot be processed as a routine refill.

EARLY REFILL REQUESTS
Refills may be requested up to 7 days before the current supply runs out. Requests submitted too early (more than 7 days early) will be queued and processed at the appropriate time. Insurance plans typically impose a fill limit of no earlier than 75% of supply used.

MEDICATION CHANGES
If you are requesting a dose change, medication switch, or new medication, an appointment is required. Refill requests are for continuation of existing, unchanged prescriptions only.

MAIL-ORDER PHARMACY
Patients with chronic conditions may benefit from mail-order pharmacy services, which allow 90-day supplies to be delivered directly to your home. Ask your provider or pharmacist about enrolling in a mail-order program.

WHAT TO DO IF YOU MISS A DOSE
For missed dose guidance, refer to your prescription label or call 1800-MED-ASSIST. Do not double your next dose unless specifically instructed by your physician.

CONTACT
